About Chuchu Bird Online Game

Chuchu Bird is a fast-paced, free-to-play HTML5 arcade game developed by Tejash. Control ChuChu the Crow as you flap through challenging pipes and obstacles across 5 unique maps: City Skies, Jungle Day, Jungle Night, Neon Cyber, and Sunset Horizon.

How to Play Chuchu Bird

  • PC / Laptop: Press the Spacebar or click your mouse to flap and gain altitude.
  • Mobile / Tablet: Tap the screen to jump and avoid crashing into obstacles.
  • Goal: Reach the distance milestones to clear levels and collect golden coins to unlock custom character skins.

Features

  • 5 progressive map stages with dynamic backgrounds.
  • Custom skin store with exclusive character tracks (ChuChu, Cyber, Blossom, Modi).
  • Smooth performance on all mobile browsers with no downloads required.
